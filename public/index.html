About RU Stable

RU-stable is your all-in-one smart campus wallet built to simplify student life at Rutgers and beyond. We created RU-stable to give college students a clearer, more empowering view of their day-to-day spending—both on and around campus. With RU-stable, you can easily track your meal plans, dining dollars, and campus card balances, all in one intuitive dashboard. Pay for events, clubs, and campus services without juggling multiple apps, and earn rewards for positive habits along the way. More than just a wallet, RU-stable connects your financial decisions to the heartbeat of campus life—helping you stay organized, engaged, and in control of your well being.

Budgeting and spending

RU-stable's budgeting and spending tool lets you track your meal swipes, flex dollars, and regular income in one simple, real-time view.

My activity

My Activity brings together your campus life by showing a unified timeline of events you've attended, places you've checked in, and activities linked to your RUstable campus identity.

Rewards and incentives

Rewards & Incentives gives students points, streaks, and perks for positive habits like staying under budget or attending academic and campus events.
